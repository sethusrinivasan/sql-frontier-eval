1. Analytical Significance & What It Means #

Scenario & Frontier Trap
Business Scenario & Problem

Users are far more likely to click the #1 search result than the #5 result regardless of relevance. Ranking quality is measured via Discounted Cumulative Gain: DCG = SUM(relevance / log2(rank + 1)).

⚡ The Frontier Trap (Why LLMs Fail)

Simple click counts reward poorly placed relevant items unfairly.

Candidate SQL Executed by gemini-3.8-flash-low (Runtime on 1M Rows: 32.98 ms) 
SELECT query_id,
       sum(relevance_label / log2(rank_position + 1)) AS dcg_at_3
FROM search_impressions
WHERE rank_position <= 3
GROUP BY query_id;
