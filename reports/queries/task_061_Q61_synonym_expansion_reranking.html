4. Queries Executed (Candidate vs Golden Ground Truth)

Beautified & Syntax Highlighted
Candidate SQL Executed by gemini-3.8-flash-low (Runtime on 1M Rows: 211.77 ms) 
WITH expanded_terms AS
  (SELECT 'sofa' AS search_term
   UNION SELECT SYNONYM
   FROM synonym_thesaurus
   WHERE base_term = 'sofa')
SELECT f.item_id,
       f.description,
       t.search_term AS matched_alias
FROM furniture_items f
JOIN expanded_terms t ON f.description ILIKE ('%' || t.search_term || '%')
ORDER BY f.item_id;
