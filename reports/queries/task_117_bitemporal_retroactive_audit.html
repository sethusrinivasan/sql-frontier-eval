1. Analytical Significance & What It Means

Scenario & Frontier Trap
Business Scenario & Problem

Reconstructs historical corporate insurance coverage on an effective business date, as known to the system on an audit date prior to a retroactive backdated modification.

⚡ The Frontier Trap (Why LLMs Fail)

Frontier models ignore the second time dimension (system transaction time) and only filter on valid_from / valid_to. This incorrectly matches the retroactive modification ($500), which was not entered into the database until May 1st, failing regulatory compliance auditing.

Prompt / User Request Given to Model
Determine customer 501's insurance deductible:
1. Effective as of business date: 2026-04-10
2. As known by the system on system audit date: 2026-04-15 (strictly BEFORE the retroactive update entered into the database on 2026-05-01).

Required output columns:
- customer_id (INT)
- active_deductible (DECIMAL)

Order by customer_id ASC.

Beautified & Syntax Highlighted
Candidate SQL Executed by gemini-3.8-flash-low (Runtime on 1M Rows: 0.13 ms) 
SELECT customer_id,
       deductible AS active_deductible
FROM insurance_policy_bitemporal
WHERE customer_id = 501 -- Business valid-time check as of 2026-04-10

  AND DATE '2026-04-10' >= valid_from
  AND DATE '2026-04-10' < valid_to -- System audit / transaction-time check as of 2026-04-15

  AND TIMESTAMP '2026-04-15 00:00:00' >= system_from
  AND TIMESTAMP '2026-04-15 00:00:00' < system_to
ORDER BY customer_id ASC;

  AND TIMESTAMP '2026-04-15 00:00:00' >= system_from
  AND TIMESTAMP '2026-04-15 00:00:00' < system_to
