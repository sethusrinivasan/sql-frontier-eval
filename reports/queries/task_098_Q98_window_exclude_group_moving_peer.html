1. Analytical Significance & What It Means

Scenario & Frontier Trap
Business Scenario & Problem

When comparing sales across product categories or dates, analysts want to compute the average of all other groups, strictly excluding the entire current group.

⚡ The Frontier Trap (Why LLMs Fail)

Computing group baselines without excluding the current group dilutes contrast in small sample sizes.

Prompt / User Request Given to Model

4. Queries Executed (Candidate vs Golden Ground Truth)

Beautified & Syntax Highlighted
Candidate SQL Executed by gemini-3.8-flash-low (Runtime on 1M Rows: 304.29 ms) 
-- Compute baseline average across other regions strictly excluding current region group

SELECT region,
       order_id,
       sale_amount,
       round(avg(sale_amount) OVER (
                                    ORDER BY region ROWS BETWEEN UNBOUNDED PRECEDING AND UNBOUNDED FOLLOWING exclude GROUP), 2) AS other_regions_baseline_avg
FROM regional_orders
ORDER BY region,
         order_id;
