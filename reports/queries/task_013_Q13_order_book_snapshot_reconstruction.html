1. Analytical Significance & What It Means

Scenario & Frontier Trap
Business Scenario & Problem

Market microstructure researchers need to reconstruct the exact bids and asks available at an arbitrary point in time from an append-only event stream of place, cancel, and fill orders.

⚡ The Frontier Trap (Why LLMs Fail)

Summing active orders without filtering out cancellations that occurred before the target time produces a phantom order book.

4. Queries Executed (Candidate vs Golden Ground Truth)

Beautified & Syntax Highlighted
Database Engine Context: This query was executed and profiled on DuckDB 2.0 In-Memory Engine with equivalent analytical syntax and execution verification on PostgreSQL 18.7 + Extensions (Docker Compose).
Candidate SQL Executed by gemini-3.8-flash-low (Runtime on 1M Rows: 18.91 ms) 
-- Reconstruct order book state at exactly 09:30:05
WITH snapshot_events AS
  (SELECT *
   FROM order_events
   WHERE event_time <= TIMESTAMP '2026-03-01 09:30:05'),
     active_orders AS
  (SELECT order_id,
          side,
          price, -- Net quantity remaining after cancellations/fills
 sum(CASE
         WHEN event_type = 'INSERT' THEN quantity
         ELSE -quantity
     END) AS remaining_qty
   FROM snapshot_events
   GROUP BY order_id,
            side,
            price
   HAVING sum(CASE
                  WHEN event_type = 'INSERT' THEN quantity
                  ELSE -quantity
              END) > 0)
SELECT side,
       price,
       sum(remaining_qty) AS aggregate_depth
FROM active_orders
GROUP BY side,
         price
ORDER BY price DESC;
