1. Analytical Significance & What It Means

Scenario & Frontier Trap
Business Scenario & Problem

RAG systems want high-confidence matches (similarity > 0.85) to avoid hallucinations, but if no document exceeds 0.85, they need a guaranteed top-1 fallback to provide at least some context.

⚡ The Frontier Trap (Why LLMs Fail)

A hard WHERE similarity > 0.85 yields empty sets, while a raw LIMIT 5 includes irrelevant noise.

4. Queries Executed (Candidate vs Golden Ground Truth)

Beautified & Syntax Highlighted
Candidate SQL Executed by gemini-3.8-flash-low (Runtime on 1M Rows: 504.18 ms) 
-- Score items, guarantee top 1 fallback even if below 0.85 threshold
WITH scored AS
  (SELECT kb_id,
          content,
          array_cosine_similarity(vector, [0.9, 0.2, 0.1]::FLOAT[3]) AS sim_score,
          row_number() OVER (
                             ORDER BY array_cosine_similarity(vector, [0.9, 0.2, 0.1]::FLOAT[3]) DESC) AS rank_pos
   FROM knowledge_base)
SELECT kb_id,
       content,
       round(sim_score, 4) AS sim_score,
       rank_pos,
       CASE
           WHEN sim_score >= 0.85 THEN 'CONFIDENT_MATCH'
           ELSE 'FALLBACK_TOP_MATCH'
       END AS match_type
FROM scored
WHERE sim_score >= 0.85
  OR rank_pos = 1
ORDER BY rank_pos;
