1. Analytical Significance & What It Means #

Scenario & Frontier Trap
Business Scenario & Problem

To compress embeddings from 32-bit floats to 8-bit integers, engineers evaluate the reconstruction error (distance between uncompressed and quantized vector) to measure fidelity loss.

⚡ The Frontier Trap (Why LLMs Fail)

Deploying quantized embeddings without evaluating distortion leads to silent degradation in search accuracy.

4. Queries Executed (Candidate vs Golden Ground Truth) #

Beautified & Syntax Highlighted
Database Engine Context: This query was executed and profiled on DuckDB 2.0 In-Memory Engine with equivalent analytical syntax and execution verification on PostgreSQL 18.7 + Extensions (Docker Compose) and Amazon Aurora Serverless v2 PostgreSQL.
Candidate SQL Executed by gemini-3.8-flash-low (Runtime on 1M Rows: 35.18 ms) 
SELECT vector_id,
       round(array_distance(fp32_vec, quantized_vec), 6) AS l2_quantization_error,
       round(1.0 - array_cosine_similarity(fp32_vec, quantized_vec), 6) AS cosine_distortion
FROM vector_compression_test
ORDER BY l2_quantization_error DESC;
Golden Reference Ground Truth SQL (Runtime on 1M Rows: 33.39 ms)
