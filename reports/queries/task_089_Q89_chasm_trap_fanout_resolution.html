1. Analytical Significance & What It Means

Scenario & Frontier Trap
Business Scenario & Problem

When an order has multiple discounts AND multiple shipment records, joining both child tables directly to the order creates a cross product that multiplies sales figures by 4x. Pre-aggregating each child table in CTEs before joining preserves financial integrity.

⚡ The Frontier Trap (Why LLMs Fail)

Frontier models routinely join all tables before GROUP BY, causing catastrophic silent metric corruption.

Prompt / User Request Given to Model

4. Queries Executed (Candidate vs Golden Ground Truth)

Beautified & Syntax Highlighted
Candidate SQL Executed by gemini-3.8-flash-low (Runtime on 1M Rows: 127.44 ms) 
WITH customer_orders AS
  (SELECT customer_id,
          order_id,
          base_amount
   FROM orders),
     aggregated_discounts AS
  (SELECT order_id,
          sum(amount) AS total_order_discount
   FROM order_discounts
   GROUP BY order_id),
     aggregated_shipments AS
  (SELECT order_id,
          sum(fee) AS total_order_shipping
   FROM order_shipments
   GROUP BY order_id)
SELECT o.customer_id,
       sum(o.base_amount) AS total_gross_sales,
       coalesce(sum(d.total_order_discount), 0.00) AS total_discount_amount,
       coalesce(sum(s.total_order_shipping), 0.00) AS total_shipping_fees,
       sum(o.base_amount) - coalesce(sum(d.total_order_discount), 0.00) + coalesce(sum(s.total_order_shipping), 0.00) AS net_revenue
FROM customer_orders o
LEFT JOIN aggregated_discounts d ON o.order_id = d.order_id
LEFT JOIN aggregated_shipments s ON o.order_id = s.order_id
GROUP BY o.customer_id;
Golden Reference Ground Truth SQL (Runtime on 1M Rows: 133.52 ms) 
-- Correctly aggregate discounts and shipments without fan-out inflation
 WITH agg_discounts AS
  (SELECT order_id,
          sum(amount) AS total_discount
   FROM order_discounts
   GROUP BY order_id),
      agg_shipments AS
  (SELECT order_id,
          sum(fee) AS total_shipping
   FROM order_shipments
   GROUP BY order_id)
SELECT o.customer_id,
       sum(o.base_amount) AS total_gross_sales,
       coalesce(sum(d.total_discount), 0.00) AS total_discount_amount,
       coalesce(sum(s.total_shipping), 0.00) AS total_shipping_fees,
       round(sum(o.base_amount) - coalesce(sum(d.total_discount), 0.00) + coalesce(sum(s.total_shipping), 0.00), 2) AS net_revenue
FROM orders o
LEFT JOIN agg_discounts d ON o.order_id = d.order_id
LEFT JOIN agg_shipments s ON o.order_id = s.order_id
GROUP BY o.customer_id;
