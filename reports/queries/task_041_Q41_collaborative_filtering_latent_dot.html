1. Analytical Significance & What It Means

Scenario & Frontier Trap
Business Scenario & Problem

Matrix factorization (SVD / ALS) maps users and movies to latent factor vectors. Predicted rating is dot product of user vector and movie vector plus user and movie bias terms.

⚡ The Frontier Trap (Why LLMs Fail)

Omitting bias terms causes popular movies to be underrated and niche items to be overrated.

4. Queries Executed (Candidate vs Golden Ground Truth)

Beautified & Syntax Highlighted
Candidate SQL Executed by gemini-3.8-flash-low (Runtime on 1M Rows: 462.11 ms) 
-- Predict user rating: baseline (3.5) + u_bias + m_bias + (u_vec . m_vec)

SELECT u.user_id,
       m.movie_id,
       m.title,
       round(3.5 + u.user_bias + m.movie_bias + array_inner_product(u.u_vec, m.m_vec), 2) AS predicted_rating
FROM user_latent u
CROSS JOIN item_latent m
WHERE u.user_id = 1
ORDER BY predicted_rating DESC;
Golden Reference Ground Truth SQL (Runtime on 1M Rows: 395.83 ms)
