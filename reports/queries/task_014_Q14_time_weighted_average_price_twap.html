1. Analytical Significance & What It Means

Scenario & Frontier Trap
Business Scenario & Problem

TWAP measures the average price of an asset weighted by the duration of time that each price level was sustained, preventing brief price spikes from distorting valuation.

⚡ The Frontier Trap (Why LLMs Fail)

Taking simple AVG(price) biases the result towards periods with frequent tick updates rather than sustained duration.

Prompt / User Request Given to Model
TWAP measures the average price of an asset weighted by the duration of time that each price level was sustained, preventing brief price spikes from distorting valuation.

Expected output columns: symbol, twap_price, total_duration_seconds.
Results should be ordered as specified.

4. Queries Executed (Candidate vs Golden Ground Truth)

Beautified & Syntax Highlighted
Candidate SQL Executed by gemini-3.8-flash-low (Runtime on 1M Rows: 92.05 ms) 
WITH tick_durations AS
  (SELECT symbol,
          price,
          date_diff('second', tick_time, lead(tick_time) OVER (PARTITION BY symbol
                                                               ORDER BY tick_time)) AS duration_seconds
   FROM price_ticks)
SELECT symbol,
       sum(price * duration_seconds) / sum(duration_seconds) AS twap_price,
       sum(duration_seconds) AS total_duration_seconds
FROM tick_durations
WHERE duration_seconds IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY symbol
ORDER BY symbol;
Golden Reference Ground Truth SQL (Runtime on 1M Rows: 83.52 ms) 
-- Calculate 1-hour TWAP from 10:00 to 11:00
 WITH tick_durations AS
  (SELECT symbol,
          tick_time,
          price,
          date_diff('second', tick_time, lead(tick_time, 1, TIMESTAMP '2026-03-01 11:00:00') OVER (PARTITION BY symbol
                                                                                                   ORDER BY tick_time)) AS duration_seconds
   FROM price_ticks
   WHERE tick_time < TIMESTAMP '2026-03-01 11:00:00')
SELECT symbol,
       round(sum(price * duration_seconds) / sum(duration_seconds), 2) AS twap_price,
       sum(duration_seconds) AS total_duration_seconds
FROM tick_durations
GROUP BY symbol;
