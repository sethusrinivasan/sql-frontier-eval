1. Analytical Significance & What It Means

Scenario & Frontier Trap
Business Scenario & Problem

Tests SARGable timestamp filtering and strict month boundary conditions, preventing full-scan string functions and end-of-interval leaks.

⚡ The Frontier Trap (Why LLMs Fail)

Models frequently write `BETWEEN '2025-01-01' AND '2025-02-01'`, which is inclusive on both ends and mistakenly captures records occurring exactly at midnight on February 1st (leaking records into the previous month's report). Alternatively, models wrap timestamp columns in non-SARGable scalar functions (like strftime or date_part), defeating column min/max partition pruning in columnar engines.

Prompt / User Request Given to Model
Analyze `system_logs` to calculate the count of ERROR logs and distinct error messages recorded during the calendar month of January 2025.

Requirements:
1. Filter for `severity = 'ERROR'`.
2. Filter strictly for events in January 2025 (from 2025-01-01 00:00:00 up to but strictly excluding 2025-02-01 00:00:00).
3. Keep the timestamp predicate SARGable (using direct range comparisons on `event_timestamp`) so that analytical partitions can be pruned.

Required output columns:
- error_count (BIGINT or INT)
- distinct_error_messages (BIGINT or INT)

Only a single summary row is returned.

SELECT count(*) AS error_count,
       count(DISTINCT message) AS distinct_error_messages
FROM system_logs
WHERE severity = 'ERROR'
  AND event_timestamp >= TIMESTAMP '2025-01-01 00:00:00'
  AND event_timestamp < TIMESTAMP '2025-02-01 00:00:00';
