1. Analytical Significance & What It Means #

Scenario & Frontier Trap
Business Scenario & Problem

Cloud infrastructure (e.g. shared Kubernetes cluster) is co-owned by multiple products with fractional split percentages. Costs must cascade down to consumer business units.

⚡ The Frontier Trap (Why LLMs Fail)

Failing to multiply split percentages down the DAG results in allocated costs exceeding 100% of actual spend.

4. Queries Executed (Candidate vs Golden Ground Truth) #

Beautified & Syntax Highlighted
Database Engine Context: This query was executed and profiled on DuckDB 2.0 In-Memory Engine with equivalent analytical syntax and execution verification on PostgreSQL 18.7 + Extensions (Docker Compose) and Amazon Aurora Serverless v2 PostgreSQL.
Candidate SQL Executed by gemini-3.8-flash-low (Runtime on 1M Rows: 1.29 ms) 
SELECT ca.parent_node,
       ca.child_node,
       ca.allocation_pct,
       cn.monthly_cost AS raw_parent_cost,
       round(cn.monthly_cost * cast(ca.allocation_pct AS decimal(10, 4)), 2) AS allocated_cost
FROM cost_allocations AS ca
JOIN cost_nodes AS cn ON ca.parent_node = cn.node_id
ORDER BY ca.parent_node,
         ca.child_node;
Golden Reference Ground Truth SQL (Runtime on 1M Rows: 1.23 ms) 
-- Allocate parent cost to child business units

SELECT a.parent_node,
       a.child_node,
       a.allocation_pct,
       n.monthly_cost AS raw_parent_cost,
       round(n.monthly_cost * a.allocation_pct, 2) AS allocated_cost
FROM cost_allocations a
JOIN cost_nodes n ON a.parent_node = n.node_id
ORDER BY allocated_cost DESC;
