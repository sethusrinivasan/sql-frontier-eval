1. Analytical Significance & What It Means

Scenario & Frontier Trap
Business Scenario & Problem

To detect anomalous spikes, analysts compare a metric against the average of neighboring days, strictly excluding the current day's value to avoid skewing the baseline.

⚡ The Frontier Trap (Why LLMs Fail)

Computing rolling averages without excluding the current row inflates the baseline during the spike itself, hiding the anomaly.

4. Queries Executed (Candidate vs Golden Ground Truth)

Beautified & Syntax Highlighted
Candidate SQL Executed by gemini-3.8-flash-low (Runtime on 1M Rows: 307.19 ms) 
-- Calculate 5-day baseline average strictly excluding current row

SELECT service_id,
       call_date,
       total_calls, -- 2 days before and 2 days after, excluding current day
 round(avg(total_calls) OVER (PARTITION BY service_id
                              ORDER BY call_date ROWS BETWEEN 2 PRECEDING AND 2 FOLLOWING exclude CURRENT ROW), 2) AS peer_baseline_avg, -- Spikiness ratio
 round(total_calls / avg(total_calls) OVER (PARTITION BY service_id
                                            ORDER BY call_date ROWS BETWEEN 2 PRECEDING AND 2 FOLLOWING exclude CURRENT ROW), 2) AS spike_factor
FROM daily_api_calls
ORDER BY call_date;
