Scenario & Frontier Trap
Business Scenario & Problem

Legal e-discovery and patent search rely on exact Boolean syntax: (patent AND battery) NOT (lithium OR phone). Negations must be strictly applied without dropping valid candidate documents.

⚡ The Frontier Trap (Why LLMs Fail)

Poor operator precedence in SQL boolean expressions causes negated terms to drop unrelated valid results.

4. Queries Executed (Candidate vs Golden Ground Truth)

Beautified & Syntax Highlighted
Candidate SQL Executed by gemini-3.8-flash-low (Runtime on 1M Rows: 85.86 ms) 
SELECT patent_id,
       abstract
FROM patent_corpus
WHERE abstract ILIKE '%battery%'
  AND NOT (abstract ILIKE '%lithium%'
           OR abstract ILIKE '%phone%')
ORDER BY patent_id;
Golden Reference Ground Truth SQL (Runtime on 1M Rows: 93.82 ms) 
-- Query: ('battery') AND NOT ('lithium' OR 'phone')

SELECT patent_id,
       abstract
FROM patent_corpus
WHERE abstract ILIKE '%battery%'
  AND NOT (abstract ILIKE '%lithium%'
           OR abstract ILIKE '%phone%')
ORDER BY patent_id;
